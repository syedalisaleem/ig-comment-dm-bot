How it works

🗨️

1. Watch your posts

Add as many post or reel URLs as you want (one per line). It checks all of them for new comments every ~45 seconds.

📨

2. Reply, then DM

Each new commenter first gets a public reply to their comment from your account, then your custom DM. Support {username} and {comment} placeholders.

🛡️

3. Stay safe

Random delays, hourly/daily caps, pause window, and a memory file so nobody is ever replied or DM'd twice.

🔑

Keyword mode

Only act on comments containing specific words (e.g. "price", "info") — or everyone.

👤

Your own account

Runs locally on your PC with your own login. Nothing is hosted or stored on any server.

💸

Free forever

No paid API, no SaaS, no limits hidden behind a paywall.

Setup (2 minutes)

1

Open the app

Double-click IGCommentDMBot.exe. A window opens with a Bot tab (Start/Stop + live log) and a Settings tab.

2

Fill in Settings

In the Settings tab, set:

  1. Username — your Instagram username
  2. Session ID — your login cookie (see next step)
  3. Post / Reel URLs — one URL per line, as many as you want (you can add more videos later)
  4. Optional: reply text, DM template, keywords, delays and limits

Click Save Settings — everything is stored in config.json beside the app.

3

Get your sessionid

Log into instagram.com in Chrome, press F12 → Application tab → Cookies → instagram.com, copy the sessionid cookie value, and paste it into the app.

4

Press Start Bot

Leave the window open — new commenters get DMs automatically, and the built-in log shows everything that happens. Close the window to stop.

Please read: This tool automates actions Instagram's Terms of Service discourage. Use it responsibly on your own account, keep volumes low, and expect a risk of rate-limits or account checks — the built-in limits help reduce that. The default caps (5 DMs/hour, 20/day) are intentionally conservative.
